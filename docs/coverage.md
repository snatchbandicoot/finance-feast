this was written in, so the "not covered" list below comes from general knowledge of that library,
not from reading it. Compare it with the real list and tell us what is missing.

## Not covered yet, and could be

Standard studies that can be computed from one price (and volume) series, which a later pass could
add. In rough order of how often they appear:

- **Price-based:** Momentum (plain price change over N bars), Percent Change, Disparity Index,
  Price Zone Oscillator, Demand Index, Intraday Momentum Index, Double Stochastics, Schaff Trend Cycle.
- **Trend and stops:** ATR Trailing Stop and Volatility Stop (Chandelier-style exits), Moving
  Average Crossover and Ribbon, Zero-Lag EMA, Linear Regression Slope, Channel and Forecast, Standard
  Error Bands, Projection Bands and Oscillator, Polarized Fractal Efficiency, Trend Quality.
- **Structure:** ZigZag and Fractals (both depend on hindsight, so each needs a clear warning about
  repainting), Darvas Box, Swing Index and Accumulative Swing Index.
- **Other oscillators:** Woodie's CCI, DeMarker, Elder Impulse, TTM Squeeze, Alligator.
- **Cycle filters (Ehlers):** Center of Gravity, Cyber Cycle, Sine Wave, Distant Coefficient Filter.
  These are mathematically heavier and the evidence for them is thin, so each page would say that.
- **Volume:** Volume Flow Indicator.

## Not covered, and why

- **Market breadth** (Advance/Decline, McClellan Oscillator and Summation Index, Arms Index (TRIN),
  TICK, up/down volume, percent of stocks above a moving average). These need data on a whole
  market, not one instrument's prices, so they cannot be shown with this site's one-series sample data.
- **Candlestick patterns** (doji, engulfing, hammer and the like). These are pattern definitions,
  not studies with a formula and a reading. They would suit a separate section.
- **Drawing tools** (Fibonacci retracements, Andrews pitchfork, Gann tools, trendlines, Elliott
  Wave). These are manual or subjective, so there is no calculation to verify.
- **Two-series studies** (Beta, Correlation, Spread, Ratio, Relative Strength against an index).
  They need a second price series. They could be added with a second sample series.
- **Platform-specific items** (paint bars, custom scripts, order-flow and options-chain displays).
  The site avoids platform-specific material unless a study is truly tied to one.
- **Fundamental and economic data.** Out of scope for a technical-studies library.
